01 / IDENTITY

Personal, precise, quietly eccentric.

The visible wordmark is ry—lowercase, no “e”, no full stop. The domain remains ry.cool. Use Ryan Storrier for the person, and Edinburgh, Scotland for the location.

ry

Hover the wordmark. The spectrum is a small moment of personality, not the default text colour.

Lead with the things Ryan makes. Keep the biography brief. Use generous space, neutral surfaces, clear navigation and careful details. Avoid a corporate portfolio voice, oversized sales claims, neon AI styling and ornamental interface clutter.

Neutral foundations. Selective colour.

Activity and interaction

Click any swatch to copy its hex value. Reserve blue, indigo and purple for recorded activity, editorial illustrations and interaction feedback. Do not introduce green as a general interface accent.

Wordmark spectrum: #FF9F0A → #FF375F → #BF5AF2 → #5856D6 → #007AFF → #30B0C7, at 110°. The Macintosh badge uses its own six-stripe period palette.

03 / TYPOGRAPHY

System type, carefully set.

Use the native system sans-serif stack: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Segoe UI, sans-serif. On macOS this resolves to the system’s San Francisco family. No downloaded font is required.

05 / COMPONENTS

Small, consistent building blocks.

HomeProjects
Say hello ↗AI projects, early ideas, or something you’ve seen here.

Navigation

Rounded line icons: 24×24 viewBox, 1.5 px stroke, round caps and joins. Render at 21 px in the sidebar and 17 px beside inline links. Inherit the text colour. Active rows use the selected surface and aria-current.

Contact area

LinkedIn and GitHub only. Email below, then a live Edinburgh clock using Europe/London and a time-sensitive greeting. The clock handles GMT/BST automatically.

Projects ↗Articles ↗About ↗

Project cards and article rows

Project cards combine a visual cover, title, stable archive number, short description and truthful status. Numbers are identifiers, not a claim that a product has shipped. Article rows use restrained geometric artwork, a clear title, topic and reading time.

AI activity

Illustrative specimen only. The site uses real aggregated local records. Periods: 7 days and 30 days. The grid has 15 columns on desktop / 10 on mobile. A randomly selected, explicitly fictional conversion appears underneath on each visit; it is not environmental data. The “About the real numbers” disclosure retains the data methodology. Missing records have outlined cells. Each day has a readable label and a selectable breakdown. Totals include cached and repeated input; they are not a bill or a productivity score.

06 / MOTION

One entrance. No detour.

Current flow: full-viewport 3D Macintosh → homepage. Show the period “hello” screen, complete keyboard, right-hand wired mouse and rainbow badge. Drag rotates the Macintosh; clicking its screen or pressing Enter opens Home. The floating scene remains saved and disabled.

07 / VOICE & CONTENT

Thoughtful. Direct. Human.

Write in the first person, with concrete verbs and occasional dry humour. Explain what something does before describing the technology. Keep experiments and in-progress work clearly labelled. Avoid client or employer details in this personal collection.
